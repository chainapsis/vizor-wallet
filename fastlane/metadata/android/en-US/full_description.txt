Vizor is a self-custody Zcash wallet for creating, receiving, shielding, and sending ZEC without giving a hosted wallet service control over your funds.

Features include shielded Unified Addresses, transparent receiving addresses, local transaction scanning, optional Tor routing for network requests, multiple accounts, Keystone hardware-wallet support, configurable lightwalletd endpoints, and local passcode protection.

Back up your recovery phrase before using the wallet. Vizor cannot recover it for you. Transparent Zcash addresses and transactions are public on-chain.
