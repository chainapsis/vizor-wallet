# UI copy conventions

Use when changing visible labels, capitalization, or literal copy assertions.

- Use sentence case, preserving proper names/acronyms such as Vizor, Zcash,
  Keystone, ZEC, USDC, USDT, and NEAR. Interpolated symbols keep their casing;
  surrounding words remain sentence case.
- Figma display headings, sidebar entries, and screen titles may retain title
  case. Do not normalize those exceptions in broad copy sweeps.
- Update [Widgetbook fixtures](../../../../lib/widgetbook) and literal assertions in
  [tests](../../../../test) with changed copy. Historical copy-audit CSVs may exist
  locally but are not required tracked files; these conventions are the shared reference.
