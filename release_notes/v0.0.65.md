# Vizor Wallet v0.0.65

This release adds privacy options for Swap and Pay, shows when confirming funds
will be ready to spend, and simplifies new wallet setup.

## What's New

- Choose the **Confidential** route in Swap or Payment settings to keep deposits
  and payouts from being linked through NEAR Intents.
- Enable **Enhance swap privacy** to use one-time Zcash addresses for ZEC payouts
  and refunds. If a payout or refund is missing, run **Check swap history** in
  Settings.
- Choose **ZecBlock Private** in Explorer settings to look up confirmed mainnet
  transactions while keeping the transaction ID private from ZecBlock.
- Enable **Hide balance on unlock** to automatically hide amounts each time you
  unlock your wallet.

## Improvements

- Send shows how much ZEC you can spend now and estimates when confirming funds
  will become available. Received transaction details show confirmation progress.
- Creating a wallet or opening a new account to redeem a gift card takes fewer
  steps. Home guides you through backing up your secret phrase and learning
  about Zcash.
- Swap, Pay, and Contacts now check scanned QR codes against the selected
  network. Contacts supports seven more networks, and address validation now
  covers Litecoin, Dogecoin, Dash, Bitcoin Cash, TON, and Cardano.
- Swap and Pay show what to check after an incorrect or late deposit or when a
  refund is missing. You can copy the deposit details when contacting support.

## Fixes

- Fixed Keystone rejecting large signing requests when sending, voting, or
  migrating funds to Ironwood. Vizor now splits large requests into smaller
  signing rounds.
- Fixed a Tor connection error that could interrupt voting. Vizor now retries the
  connection and can switch servers when checking voting status.

## Notes

- **Enhance swap privacy** is available on mainnet for software accounts. With
  this setting enabled, sending ZEC through Swap or Pay uses your Ironwood
  balance. Preparing a one-time address can take a few minutes.
