# Vizor Wallet v0.0.59

This release adds experimental private transaction queries and makes
gift card and swap outcomes clearer.

## What's New

- Added experimental Private queries on mainnet. Enable it in Settings
  or before importing a wallet to look up supported transactions without
  revealing their IDs to servers.

## Improvements

- Improved mainnet restoration privacy by calculating wallet birthday
  dates locally and starting restored wallets from broader block ranges.
- Reduced the date-based import safety window from 15 days to seven days
  to limit extra scanning.
- Gift cards claimed in another wallet now appear in a separate
  "Claimed elsewhere" group and can be removed.
- Clearer Swap and Pay results distinguish failures from recorded refunds
  and show refunded amounts and fees when available.
- Swap deposit screens now identify the accepted token and network,
  and slippage settings explain what the percentage controls.
- Received transaction details no longer show fees paid by the sender.
  Wallets with many transparent receipts also download less data during
  their initial sync.
- Changed the default block explorer to ZecBlock.

## Fixes

- Fixed memo-only sends and receives appearing incorrectly or missing
  from Activity. They now show their memos and zero-value amounts.
- Added detection of funds returned after TEX sends and recovery of
  funds left behind when a follow-up transaction expires.
- Fixed repeated cancelled Ledger or Keystone approvals eventually
  blocking further TEX sends.
- Improved recovery when an account import is interrupted while saving
  its setup, preserving the imported account and unlock credential.

## Notes

- Private queries are off by default and available only on mainnet.
  Transaction details may take longer to appear. Enabling the setting
  does not rescan existing history or undo earlier disclosures.
- Newly restored mainnet wallets may show an earlier birthday and scan
  additional blocks as part of the privacy improvement.
