# Vizor Wallet v0.0.58

This release introduces gift card groups, improves gift card privacy and
claiming, and makes hardware wallet signing easier.

## What's New

- Create gift cards for a group in one transaction and save all links as
  a CSV file. Create up to 50 cards with a software account, or up to 30
  with Keystone or Ledger, with the same amount and optional message.

## Improvements

- Faster gift card claim preparation, with claims available after two
  confirmations.
- Improved gift card privacy: claim transactions no longer expose the
  recipient address through the card's recovery information, and usage
  checks no longer request individual funding transactions from the server.
- Gift cards are now organized into pending, unused, and used sections.
- Password fields now warn when Caps Lock is on.
- Clearer Ledger error messages explain how to recover. Ledger Zcash app
  3.9.4 and later also supports signing non-English and multiline memos,
  shown as a hash on the device.

## Fixes

- Fixed gift card amounts remaining visible in lists with privacy mode on.
- Fixed receive-address QR codes that Keystone devices could not scan,
  and made signing QR animation consistent across flows.
- Fixed Ledger voting failures and allowed access to voting before all
  Orchard funds have migrated, when eligible Ironwood funds are available.
- Fixed transaction IDs in activity details to match block explorers.
- Improved recovery after interrupted syncing to avoid unnecessarily
  resubmitting previously confirmed transactions.
- Fixed slow app shutdown and a Windows crash during exit.

## Notes

- Connecting a new Ledger account requires Ledger Zcash app 3.9.4 or later.
  Previously connected accounts can still sign with 3.9.3, but non-English
  and multiline memos require 3.9.4 or later.
