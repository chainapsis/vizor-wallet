# Vizor Wallet v0.0.55

This release introduces ZEC gift cards and payment requests, improves
address copying and Tor reliability, and fixes balance issues after
cancelling Keystone signing.

## What's New

- Create ZEC gift cards with a design and personal message, share them
  by link, and redeem received cards into your chosen account.
- Create payment requests with an amount and optional memo, share them
  as links or QR codes, and review incoming requests before sending.

## Improvements

- Full addresses are now easier to read and copy.
- Improved reliability when loading prices and linking wallets over Tor.
- Added checks for previously used voting rights after restoring a wallet,
  helping prevent attempts to reuse them.

## Fixes

- Fixed zero or insufficient balances appearing after cancelling Keystone
  signing. Send review now preserves your inputs and refreshes balances
  and fees before retrying.
