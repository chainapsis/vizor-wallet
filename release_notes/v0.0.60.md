# Vizor Wallet v0.0.60

This release refreshes desktop wallet setup, expands experimental Private
queries to transparent funds, and improves gift cards, transaction details,
and sync progress.

## What's New

- Redeem gift cards while creating or importing a wallet, or scan a gift card
  QR code from Settings.
- Enter gift card amounts in USD or ZEC, with the exact ZEC amount preserved
  when switching currencies.
- Expanded Private queries on mainnet to recover transparent funds and history
  for software, Keystone, and Ledger accounts without public address lookups.
- Payment links opened from other apps now show a warning before request
  details, with an option to scan the original QR code before reviewing payment.

## Improvements

- Ironwood gift cards become ready to claim without waiting for a full scan.
- Transaction details open immediately and load additional information in
  place. Activity now distinguishes Orchard and Sapling transactions and shows
  clearer pending estimates and connection status.
- Improved transparent transaction details as recovery completes, keeping
  unavailable recipient and fee information clearly marked as unknown.
- Removed deprecated assets from new Swap and Pay selections and added missing
  token and network logos.
- Ledger now verifies the connected account before sharing transaction data
  for signing. Gift card creation shows and enforces a four-card batch limit.

## Fixes

- Fixed sync progress freezing after automatic retries. Final rescans now
  appear as a separate "Checking" stage.
- Improved recovery of interrupted Ledger and Keystone transactions that have
  already confirmed, avoiding unnecessary resubmission or signing.
- Fixed gift card claims becoming stuck after a blockchain reorganization.
- Improved recovery from interrupted wallet setup and prevented incoming links
  from disrupting setup, signing, or scanning.

## Notes

- Private queries remain experimental and mainnet-only. If already enabled,
  they now also apply to transparent recovery. Transparent funds cannot be
  spent or shielded until private recovery confirms their availability; Vizor
  does not fall back to public lookups when the private service is unavailable.
- This update upgrades the wallet database. Downgrading an upgraded wallet to
  an earlier app version is unsupported.
