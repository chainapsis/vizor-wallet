# Vizor Wallet v0.0.57

This release improves Ledger connections and signing, shortens gift card
links, and fixes balance and transaction history issues after interrupted
signing or account recovery.

## Improvements

- Shorter gift card links make sharing easier while preserving card designs
  and personal messages.
- Clearer Ledger signing progress shows when to wait and when to approve,
  with waiting guidance tailored to your device.
- On macOS, choose USB or Bluetooth for each Ledger operation. Bluetooth
  device selection and pairing recovery are clearer, and saved devices
  skip repeated viewing-key approval.
- Accounts with unshared gift cards can now be removed after a warning
  to copy any links you still need.

## Fixes

- Fixed an issue that caused Ledger to reject some transactions.
- Improved Ledger Bluetooth recovery on macOS after failed connections
  or stalled device checks.
- Fixed reduced spendable balances after closing Vizor while Ledger or
  Keystone approval was pending.
- Fixed missing amounts and transaction details when reimporting an account
  whose transactions were also stored for another account.

## Notes

- Recipients need Vizor v0.0.57 or newer to open newly shared gift card
  links. Existing gift card links remain supported.
- Transaction details already missing from a previously reimported account
  may require another rescan or reimport to recover.
