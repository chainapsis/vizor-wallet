# Vizor Wallet v0.0.64

This release adds Korean and Simplified Chinese, automatic mainnet server
selection, and faster wallet syncing, with improvements to Swap, Pay, and
hardware wallet reliability.

## What's New

- Choose Korean or Simplified Chinese from the language picker on Welcome
  or in Settings.
- Automatic mainnet server selection chooses a healthy, responsive server
  and shows the selected server in Settings.
- Swap and Pay show dismissible notices about NEAR Intents service issues
  and maintenance affecting the selected chains.
- Added the Rogue cat gift card design.

## Improvements

- Faster wallet restores and transaction history syncing.
- More consistent sync progress that resumes after restarting or unlocking,
  with a simpler status display for short catch-ups.
- Pay now has Payment progress and Transaction details tabs. Open NEAR
  Intents Explorer from Swap and Pay activity while a transaction is still
  processing.

## Fixes

- Fixed saved Swap activity being lost when switching accounts or updating
  activity before earlier records finished loading.
- Fixed amounts remaining visible with Hide balance enabled in gift card
  creation, Swap and Pay records, Ironwood migration, and voting.
- Fixed Ledger Bluetooth reconnection incorrectly reporting an account
  mismatch for some existing accounts.
- Keystone QR scanning now recovers after an unreadable payload without
  requiring you to leave and reopen the screen.
- Improved tracking of Ledger and Keystone payments after a server rejection,
  keeping submitted payments pending until confirmed or expired to reduce
  the risk of duplicate payments.
- Removed an extra block of waiting before reusing change, claiming gift
  cards, or continuing an Ironwood migration.
- Fixed voting rounds with post-NU7 snapshots being rejected and participation
  verification failing after validator changes.
- Fixed a crash when closing the app on Linux.
